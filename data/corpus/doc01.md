# Household income limits

Synthetic policy for the Meridian lab. Gross monthly income must be at or below 130 percent of the federal poverty level for the household size. For a household of one the limit is 1,632 USD; two, 2,215 USD; three, 2,798 USD; four, 3,380 USD. Add 583 USD for each additional member. Net income after deductions must be at or below 100 percent of the poverty level. Caseworkers recheck the income limit at every recertification.
