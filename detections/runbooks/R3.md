# R3: injection spike

**Rule:** CloudWatch alarm `meridian-injection-spike` on the `GuardrailIntervened` metric, built from `guardrail_intervened` lines in `/aws/lambda/meridian-app`. Fires at 5 or more in 10 minutes. It is an alarm, not a Sentinel rule, because the Sentinel S3 connector does not ingest Lambda logs. It has no notification action (POA&M), so someone has to look at it.

## What it means
The guard layer blocked several requests in a short time: a person or script is probing the assistant with prompt attacks, or an indirect injection is being retrieved repeatedly.

## First checks
1. Which caller and which category? The log line carries both, never content:
   `aws logs filter-log-events --log-group-name /aws/lambda/meridian-app --filter-pattern guardrail_intervened`
2. One caller or many? One caller points at a person; many at a poisoned document.
3. `stage` shows where it was caught: `input` (the question), `tool:<name>` (a tool result) or `output`.
4. Did anything get through? Look for `tool_denied` lines and answers that carry a canary or an off-list link.

## Containment
1. If one caller: revoke that user's access and review their earlier requests.
2. If a document: remove it from the corpus bucket and review who could write to it.
3. Do not turn the guard layer down to stop the alarm.

## False positives
A test run (`tests/run.py`) or a demo. Check the time against your own simulations first.
